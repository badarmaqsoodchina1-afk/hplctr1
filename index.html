# About Me

🔐

Postdoctoral researcher specialising in cybersecurity and trustworthy AI for cyber-physical and distributed systems, with a focus on securing critical infrastructures and large-scale digital environments.

Published 27 research papers including multiple first-author contributions in leading Q1 venues such as IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, and IEEE Internet of Things Journal.

Current research focuses on LLM-driven malware detection, zero-day attack analysis, AI-based vulnerability discovery, and federated/privacy-preserving learning for distributed environments.
